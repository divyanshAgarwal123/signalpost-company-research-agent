# Signalpost prize strategy — 15 September 2026

The [Builderr board](https://builderr.ai/challenges/signalpost) has five submissions, four provisional scores and **no confirmed qualified entry** as of its 13 September review. Ajai and Penge exceed 65 but miss minimum coverage; Anmol exceeds 65 but has a wrong-company website. This is a real opening only if our agent clears **all** qualification gates, not just a raw score. A 100-company live local batch and a preliminary 1,000-profile artifact passed structural checks, yet the latter published only 41 exact-site claims. External company recall, exact-entity precision and a numeric Builderr score remain unknown. [The measured readiness assessment](EVALUATION_ASSESSMENT.md) finds high failure risk and no proven qualification if submitted now.

The distinctive product should be a **company relationship and change trail keyed by organisation number**. It must separate the legal company from its workplace subunits, brands, parents and websites; prove each relationship from registry, company-owned pages or source payloads; attach dated official jobs and company posts only through that proof; and show a claim-level timeline of new, unchanged, expired and withdrawn information. This is an implementation target, not a claim of uniqueness: [Rakesh's public project](https://github.com/Rakesh-Tummala/signalpost-company-agent) already describes search-led deep crawling, and [Kildespor's public project](https://github.com/officialarghya29/kildespor) already emphasizes evidence chains and reports 8.6% gated website coverage and no jobs on its own sample. Their repos do not prove official Board identity or final results. Our current integrated [Merchant control](https://arbeidsplassen.nav.no/stillinger/stilling/066dd2e6-7695-43fd-bd84-6e4482c72e32) adds an **active job under a legally linked workplace**, an exact company site and a dated company article in one profile. It is one positive control, not evidence that we cover most checked positive companies.

The main pool pays **$1,200/$500/$300** for first/second/third qualified technical entries. One entry cannot take all three ranks. A separate **$250/$150/$100 JBOX** pool is listed for qualified JBOX-built entries, but Builderr does not publish its provenance proof or whether one entrant can stack both pools. The **$100 community votes** are scheduled 20 September, 4 October and 18 October after the passed 6 September vote; only qualified entries enter the hosted gallery, and voting never changes the technical rank. Five days remain until 20 September, so that vote is a stretch target requiring a qualified artifact and gallery first. The realistic monetary objective is first qualified technical rank, then independently verify JBOX and community eligibility. [Builderr's published rubric](https://builderr.ai/challenges/signalpost) gives 35 coverage, 30 accuracy/evidence, 20 refresh, 10 synthesis and 5 UX points, plus separate 65 overall/21 coverage/60% external recall/95% external precision gates. Its checked reference union and field weights are private, so no honest local dollar probability or numeric score exists yet.
