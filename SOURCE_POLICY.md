# Signalpost source, cost and access declarations

Current local checks, 15 September 2026. This is the audit record for the agent's sources; it is **not** a signed rights approval or frozen submission declaration.

| Source | Use in the code | Access and publication boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Brønnøysund open Enhetsregister API](https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/api/dokumentasjon/en/index.html) and supplied public bulk snapshot | Anchor organisation numbers, registered identity, leadership, legal subunits and related structures. Its documentation lists the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD 2.0). | Only the public endpoints are used; the authorised person-number endpoint is excluded. Role normalization excludes dates of birth. A live response with another embedded organisation number is ambiguous, not a company claim. |
| [Brønnøysund public annual-accounts API](https://data.brreg.no/regnskapsregisteret/regnskap/swagger-ui/swagger-ui/index.html?urls.primaryName=aarsregnskap) | Filed financial fields with record reporting periods and embedded legal entity. | The accounts response must embed the exact requested organisation number in each accepted record. Do not assume the Enhetsregister NLOD statement automatically covers every annual-account document; verify the separate publication conditions before a public raw-snapshot archive. |
| Exact company-owned website | A registry-listed or licensed-search-discovered site is a candidate; homepage and at most a bounded set of legal, contact, careers and news pages can become evidence after fetched-page identity. | Private/local/reserved hosts and redirects are blocked. An unavailable robots file permits at most one ordinary homepage GET; deeper pages require robots permission. A brand, group or manager site is withheld unless it proves this exact legal company. No scraped search-result snippets become claims. |
| [NAV authenticated job feed](https://navikt.github.io/pam-stilling-feed/) and [use terms](https://arbeidsplassen.nav.no/vilkar-api) | ACTIVE and unexpired adverts are checked against the employer organisation number or a Brønnøysund-linked workplace subunit. Recent INACTIVE feed events remove known UUIDs from current publication. | NAV is free but a stable private consumer JWT needs registration and written terms agreement. The rotating public token is used **only for local experiments**. The output stores job title, ID, dates and link; it excludes NAV's `contactList` and description containing personal data. A bounded feed absence cannot prove that older jobs do not exist. |
| [Brave Search API](https://brave.com/search/api/) | Optional licensed candidate URL discovery only, never claim evidence. | A server-side key is required and absent here. Search pricing is $5 per 1,000 queries with $5 free monthly credits; report declares $0.005/query before credits rather than relying on a promotional balance. This is $0.50 for 100 queries; fetched-page identity still controls publication. |

Current models/API services: **no hosted model is used** in the terminal claim or company identity path. Dependency versions are pinned locally. Keys are accepted only through server-side `BRAVE_SEARCH_API_KEY` and `NAV_JOB_FEED_TOKEN`; the experimental NAV JWT is transient and never committed. The latest code counts official retries and redirects, NAV feed/detail attempts and redirects, and company-site robots/page redirects; Brave redirects are rejected so its server-side key stays on the pinned API origin. The entered version-one default enables neither connector. Its final 100-company run returned 100/100 results in 422 seconds with 647 reported requests and $0 declared spend; those local budget checks pass. A separate network-boundary audit, individual company-site reuse licences and the optional connectors' true redirect/retry budgets remain unverified. The public artifact removes unclaimed website bodies and full annual-account copies; it publishes sourced facts and short proof spans from the allowed official/public pages. A lawful six-month NAV index with incremental updates is a future requirement, not enabled in that default batch. Daily evaluation allows 100 inputs, 45 minutes, 2,000 outbound requests including retries and redirects, and at most $10 declared third-party spend. [Builderr's source rules](https://builderr.ai/starter-briefs/signalpost-sources.md) and [evaluation contract](https://builderr.ai/docs/signalpost-evaluation-harness.md) remain the authority for final rights and qualification review.
